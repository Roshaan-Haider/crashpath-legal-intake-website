We answer the calls law firms cannot reach.

CrashPath operates the intake and lead-qualification layer between marketing campaigns and practicing law firms. We ensure every inbound caller receives immediate, structured attention.

Founded on the call nobody answered

CrashPath was founded in 2016 in Austin, TX after examining how high-volume personal injury campaigns lose prospective clients. Firms were spending substantial marketing budgets generating calls, only to have prospects encounter busy signals, untrained answering services, or voicemail queues.

We built CrashPath as a dedicated operational bridge. We do not act as lead brokers, and we do not provide legal counsel. Instead, we staff dedicated campaign queues with rigorously trained agents who execute strict screening scripts and transfer complete case files directly to reviewing legal teams.

How a call is handled and audited

Quality assurance is not an afterthought at CrashPath. Every single interaction passes through a structured handling and review cycle.

  1. 1

    Direct recording and consent capture

    Every inbound call begins with jurisdiction-specific recording notices and disclosure statements. Audio capture records continuously while speech-to-text systems generate timestamped transcripts.

  2. 2

    Real-time criteria scoring

    As the agent moves through the custom screening branch, responses are scored against the firm qualification benchmarks. Qualified leads transfer instantly with priority indicators.

  3. 3

    Independent QA auditing

    Dedicated quality review analysts sample calls daily. Audits verify verbatim disclosure reading, accurate factual logging, empathetic tone control, and strict refusal to offer legal guidance.

  4. 4

    Weekly agent calibration

    Operations supervisors hold weekly script calibration reviews. Any agent whose script adherence dips below 92% is placed into retraining before returning to live queues.

Our operating principles

We define our organizational values by observable behaviors on every live phone call.

Strict adherence

Concrete behavior: No intake begins without recorded caller consent

Agents never skip required disclosures to speed up handle times. If a caller declines recording consent, the agent ends the screening respectfully and logs the preference without exception.

Zero legal opinions

Concrete behavior: Explicit redirect on all legal inquiries

When callers ask whether their accident warrants compensation or if their case is strong, agents explicitly state that only licensed attorneys make that evaluation and return strictly to factual intake.

Respect for caller distress

Concrete behavior: Immediate pause when distress is detected

If an injured caller exhibits physical pain or emotional overwhelm, agents immediately stop the checklist, check that the caller is safe, and offer to schedule a callback rather than rushing questions.

Full data transparency

Concrete behavior: Verbatim delivery with unedited recordings

Every delivered lead package contains complete raw call audio and word-for-word transcripts. We never summarize caller statements or omit awkward exchanges from client records.
