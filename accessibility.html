1. Accessibility commitment

CrashPath strives to make its web presence usable and accessible to visitors of all abilities, including individuals who navigate using screen readers, keyboard-only interfaces, or speech recognition software.

We aim to align our digital experiences with recognized accessibility principles: ensuring content is perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust across modern desktop and mobile browsers.

2. Implemented features

During the design and construction of this demonstration website, we implemented the following technical practices:

  • Semantic HTML structure: Content uses logical landmark elements (header, nav, main, footer, section), clear heading hierarchies (single h1 with ordered subheadings), and ordered lists for process steps.
  • Keyboard navigation & focus indicators: All interactive elements, including navigation links, dropdown toggles, and form inputs, are accessible via standard Tab key navigation with visible, high-contrast focus rings.
  • Form accessibility: Inputs feature explicit label associations, aria-required tags on mandatory fields, and aria-describedby bindings linking inputs directly to inline error text.
  • Motion preferences: Layouts respect the prefers-reduced-motion media query, disabling continuous animations and instant-rendering timeline diagrams when reduced motion is requested.
  • Contrast standards: Core text colors (deep ink on warm ivory or white surfaces) are calibrated to maintain comfortable contrast ratios for readability.

3. Formal audit status

This demonstration codebase has not yet undergone formal third-party certification or comprehensive manual audit with multiple assistive technology screen readers.

We do not claim total or flawless conformance with specific statutory accessibility frameworks. Ongoing refinement and remediation remain central to our development process as new pages and campaign tools are added.
