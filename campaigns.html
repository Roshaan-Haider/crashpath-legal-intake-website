Campaign lines that convert, not abandon.

CrashPath provides dedicated inbound call qualification for law firms and marketing agencies running high-volume paid media campaigns. Every line connects directly to a live, trained agent.

Built for teams that buy intake volume

This service is designed specifically for personal injury firms, mass tort practices, and performance marketing agencies driving paid search, social, or broadcast calls. Running paid traffic to general office lines or unmonitored queues leads to missed calls and wasted media spend.

CrashPath pairs each marketing campaign with isolated inbound phone numbers and custom qualification paths. When callers dial your campaign number, agents answer under your specific program identity and begin the screening workflow immediately.

What every campaign package includes

Dedicated campaign infrastructure

Every active campaign receives dedicated inbound DID routing, custom script branching, real-time audio recording, instantaneous transcription delivery, and direct data integration into your CRM or case management platform.

Live QA and script tracking

Call recordings are monitored against state-specific recording consent laws, disclosure scripts, and client qualification thresholds. Weekly scoring reports provide clear transparency into call handling quality.

How a pilot works

Before committing to long-term call allocations, we run a controlled onboarding test to calibrate scripts, technical handoffs, and lead scoring thresholds.

  1. 1

    Scope definition

    We define expected call volume, operating hours, target geographic territories, and technical endpoint requirements for CRM handoffs.

  2. 2

    Criteria sign-off

    Your legal and marketing teams sign off on verbatim scripts, consent disclosures, disqualification logic, and lead scoring weights.

  3. 3

    Test period

    Live campaign traffic routes to trained agents across a bounded 14-day sample of 100 calls to evaluate real-world handling and conversion.

  4. 4

    Performance review

    We conduct a joint audit of call recordings, transcript accuracy, and lead retention rates to confirm full production readiness.
