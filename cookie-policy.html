1. Current demo status

In its current demonstration deployment, this website does not set third-party tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.

The site uses only browser session storage to remember interface states, such as whether a visitor has dismissed the top announcement banner during an active browsing session. This session storage item is stored locally on your device and expires automatically when your browser tab closes.

2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to ensure websites function efficiently, remember navigation preferences, and provide analytical reporting to website administrators.

3. Future cookie categories

When this website launches into production with marketing attribution or customer relationship tools, it may implement cookies across the following standard functional categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Required to enable core navigation, form token verification, and security controls. The site cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Functional & Preference Cookies: Used to remember visitor preferences, such as selected region or previously submitted inquiry metadata.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Used to gather aggregated, non-personally identifiable visit statistics, such as page load speed, bounce rates, and traffic sources, to help optimize site navigation.
  • Marketing & Attribution Cookies: Set by advertising partners to measure campaign referral effectiveness and verify which marketing channel referred an inbound caller.

4. Managing browser preferences

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through their settings menus. You can configure your browser to notify you when a cookie is placed, reject all third-party cookies, or clear existing cookies upon browser exit.

Disabling essential cookies may impair certain interactive features, such as form validation or sticky navigation states.
