1. No legal advice provided

CrashPath is an administrative call intake and qualification company. We are not a law firm, attorney referral service, or legal aid agency. The materials published on this website and communications from our intake personnel are for general informational purposes only and do not constitute legal advice.

You should not act or refrain from acting on the basis of any content found on this website without seeking independent legal advice from an attorney licensed in your state or jurisdiction.

2. No guarantee of case acceptance or outcome

Submitting an intake form or completing a screening phone call with CrashPath does not guarantee that any attorney or law firm will review, accept, or take action on your potential legal claim.

CrashPath makes no warranties, representations, or guarantees regarding the viability, monetary value, or ultimate outcome of any motor vehicle accident claim or legal proceeding. Past legal settlements or recoveries achieved by independent law firms do not guarantee future results.

3. Intake is not legal representation

Engaging in an intake screening conversation with CrashPath does not establish an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is created solely when you and an independent, licensed attorney enter into an explicit written retainer or representation agreement.

Strict time limits known as statutes of limitations apply to personal injury and vehicle collision claims. Delaying the retention of a licensed attorney can extinguish your right to seek legal remedies.

5. Attorney advertising rules

State bar associations and state supreme courts maintain detailed ethical rules concerning legal marketing, client solicitation, and public disclosures. In some jurisdictions, website content relating to legal intake or claim evaluation may be considered attorney advertising.

Before launching marketing campaigns utilizing CrashPath intake lines, all consumer-facing disclosures, scripts, and promotional landing pages must be independently reviewed and approved by licensed attorneys in each target state.
