## Positioning statement

CrashPath runs the intake and lead-qualification calls that law firms do not have the capacity to handle themselves. We answer motor vehicle accident campaign lines, work callers through a compliant intake script, score each lead against the firm's criteria, and hand off a complete file — recording, transcript, qualification score, and intake form — before the attorney picks up the phone.

We are not a law firm. We are not a marketing agency. We are the operations layer between a claimant's first call and a firm's first case review.

## Services

1. **MVA Intake** — End-to-end intake for motor vehicle accident claimants. Covers accident type, injury status, insurance coverage, liability questions, and compliant consent language.
2. **Inbound call center** — Live agents on campaign lines, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. *(sample)*
3. **Call transcription** — Verbatim transcripts of every intake call, tagged by attorney, case type, and consent status.
4. **Final expense intake** — Lead qualification for final expense insurance campaigns.
5. **Medicare intake** — Compliant Medicare Advantage and supplement intake calls.
6. **Mortgage intake** — Qualification calls for mortgage refinance and purchase campaigns.
7. **Debt settlement intake** — Intake and qualification for debt resolution campaigns.

## Voice and tone rules

1. **Plain language.** Write at an 8th-grade reading level. No Latin phrases, no legalese outside compliance disclosures.
2. **Active voice.** "Agents answer calls" not "Calls are answered by agents."
3. **No banned words.** The following words must not appear in copy: elevate, seamless, unlock, cutting-edge, tailored solutions, foster, showcase, vibrant, pivotal, underscore, testament, landscape, tapestry, game-changer, revolutionize, harness, leverage (as a verb), synergy, robust (unless describing a data feed).
4. **No em dashes.** Use a comma, a period, or a colon instead.
5. **No inline-header lists.** Do not use bold word followed by colon at the start of a list item ("**Benefit:** Description here"). Write full sentences.
6. **No icon-topped feature cards.** Visual hierarchy comes from type, whitespace, and structure -- not decorative icon grids.
7. **Numbers are placeholders.** Every statistic uses `[XX]%` or `[X]k+` format and carries a small `(sample)` tag. No invented numbers, no implied real data.
8. **Testimonials are clearly labeled.** Every testimonial block carries "SAMPLE TESTIMONIAL -- Replace before launch" in accent text above the quote. No invented client names or firms.
9. **No real people.** Do not name any real attorney, bar association, case management vendor, or insurance carrier.
10. **Confident but not boastful.** State what the service does, not how great it is.

## Placeholder policy

| Placeholder | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[XX]%` | A percentage stat (accuracy, satisfaction, etc.) |
| `[X]k+` | A large round number (call volume, etc.) |
| `[XX]` | A number (states, days, etc.) |
| `[X]h` or `[X]s` | A time duration |
| `[City, ST]` | A location placeholder |
| `[Replace with real quote]` | Testimonial placeholder |

Every placeholder value must be verified and replaced with real, documented data before the site goes live. All stat blocks must retain the `(sample)` tag until real data is cited with a source.
