## 6. Testimonials & Outcomes Policy

Per design and compliance rules:
- All testimonials retain the prominent `SAMPLE TESTIMONIAL: Replace before launch` header.
- Outcome claims (e.g. `[XX]% increase in conversion`) have been replaced with descriptive operational statements focusing on process benefits (live coverage, transcript availability, criteria filtering).
- No invented law firm names, individual attorneys, settlement figures, or court outcomes are used anywhere on the site.
- Six legal policy documents (`privacy-policy.html`, `terms-and-conditions.html`, `cookie-policy.html`, `accessibility.html`, `call-recording-consent.html`, `disclaimer.html`) retain generic legal placeholders (`[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`, `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`, etc.) for production legal counsel completion.
