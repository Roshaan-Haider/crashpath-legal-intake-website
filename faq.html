Frequently asked questions

Operational, script configuration, and CRM integration details personal injury law firms review before onboarding an intake campaign line.

Operational questions and answers

How quickly can a new MVA campaign line go live? ↓

Standard intake lines go live within 3 business days following script approval. Custom CRM field mapping and multi-state routing take 2 additional business days. (sample)

Can we customize the screening criteria for our practice? ↓

Yes. Your firm sets all qualification rules, including statute of limitations thresholds, injury treatment minimums, vehicle categories, and target counties.

How do call recordings and transcripts transfer to our system? ↓

Records deliver via webhook, API connector, or secure email in under 10 minutes from call completion, with immediate notifications for priority files.

What happens when a caller does not qualify? ↓

Unqualified calls end respectfully with no pressure. Operators log a brief disposition note explaining which criteria were not met, so your firm retains complete marketing documentation.

What hours do your intake operators cover? ↓

CrashPath operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year across 32 states. Campaign lines receive live answers at 2 a.m. with the same adherence standard as mid-day dials. (sample)
