How an accident call is handled

From first ring to signed retainer file, every step is documented, time-stamped, and delivered to your team.

01

02

Qualification

Agent works through your approved intake script: accident type, injury status, insurance coverage, liability indicators. Calibrated with your firm before go-live.

03

Scoring and file build

Intake data scored against your firm's criteria. Audio recording and transcript generated simultaneously.

04

Handoff

Qualified lead pushed directly into your case management software. Warm transfer or notification sent to your intake attorney.
