How an accident call is handled

From initial ring to case system handoff, every call moves through four disciplined operational stages.

  1. 1

    Immediate live answer

    A trained intake agent answers your campaign number with a 2-minute average answer time, gives the required recording disclosure, and logs caller consent.

  2. 2

    Accident qualification

    The agent collects incident date, collision location, vehicle damage, injuries reported at intake, and checks whether the caller has retained legal counsel.

  3. 3

    Criteria score

    Responses are scored against your firm thresholds in real time. Priority collision cases generate instant SMS or email alerts to your on-call team.

  4. 4

    File handoff

    The structured intake form, full audio recording, and verbatim transcript transfer directly into your case management CRM under 10 minutes from call completion.

What lands in your case management system

Your intake staff receives complete, actionable records rather than messy call notes. Data fields map directly into major legal CRM platforms.

Claimant & incident details

  • Full claimant name, phone number, and email address
  • Incident date, state, city, and collision venue type
  • Vehicle damage severity and police accident report status
  • Driver, passenger, pedestrian, or rideshare involvement role
  • Affirmative recording consent timestamp

Medical, insurance & incident files

  • Reported physical injuries and emergency transport details
  • Treatment provider category (ER, hospital, chiropractic, none)
  • Caller auto insurance carrier and third-party coverage details
  • Unrepresented status (no prior counsel retained)
  • Attached high-fidelity MP3 recording and verbatim text transcript

What we don't do

  • We do not give legal advice. Agents do not assess liability, discuss settlement viability, or comment on claim strength. Any legal question redirects to the reviewing firm.
  • We do not promise case outcomes or settlement sums. Agents are strictly scripted to avoid financial representations or guarantees of compensation.
  • We do not pressure callers. If an injured caller wants to pause or stop, the conversation ends immediately. Partial details are preserved without coercion.
  • We do not contact individuals who did not ask to be contacted. All screening begins strictly from inbound dials to your published campaign lines.
