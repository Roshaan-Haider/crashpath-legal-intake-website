What we don't do

No auto-dialers, no legal advice, no pressure. Operators follow the screening criteria your firm provides and pass everything else to your attorneys.

  • We do not give legal advice. Operators do not assess legal fault or discuss claim value. Legal questions are referred directly to your firm.
  • We do not promise outcomes or settlement amounts. Intake scripts strictly prohibit financial projections or settlement promises.
  • We do not pressure callers. If an injured caller needs to pause or disconnect, the call ends immediately with details saved.
  • We do not place outbound cold calls. Every intake starts strictly from an inbound dial to your firm's campaign line.
