1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly through our website contact forms and telephone intake lines operated on behalf of marketing partners and legal practices.

This includes:

  • Contact identifiers such as your first name, last name, phone number, email address, and physical location.
  • Inbound telephone audio recordings and verbatim speech transcripts generated during intake calls.
  • Factual details regarding motor vehicle accidents, injury categories, medical care status, and auto insurance coverage.
  • Basic website technical information, including standard browser type, device category, and server request logs.

2. Why we collect it

We use collected data solely to perform call screening, record verification, and intake routing.

Specific operational purposes include:

  • Screening caller inquiries against predefined intake criteria established by participating law firms.
  • Generating verifiable case files with attached audio recordings and text transcripts for attorney review.
  • Conducting internal quality assurance audits, script calibration checks, and quality monitoring.
  • Responding directly to operational inquiries submitted through website contact forms.

3. Who we share data with

CrashPath is an intake operations vendor. We transfer collected screening files directly to the law firms or campaign partners whose marketing lines you contacted.

We also share data with contracted technical vendors who provide essential infrastructure, including telecommunication carriers, speech-to-text transcription services, and secure cloud storage hosting. These service providers process data under strict confidentiality agreements. We do not sell your personal information or license it to third-party data brokers.

6. Children's privacy

Our website and intake services are directed exclusively to adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or maintain personal records from minors. If we discover that personal details from an individual under 18 have been gathered without verifiable parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that data.
