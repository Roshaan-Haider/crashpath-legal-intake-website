What we run, and how.

CrashPath answers inbound campaign lines across seven intake programs. Each is staffed by trained agents, documented in full, and handed off to your team in a format you can act on immediately.

Every program CrashPath runs shares a common foundation: a live agent answers, works through a documented intake script, qualifies the caller against your criteria, and delivers the full record to your system before the call ends. The specific intake questions match each case type. The standard for how every call is answered, recorded, and delivered does not change.

One operation, seven programs

Every agent who answers a CrashPath campaign line completes the same training foundation before taking live calls. The script calibration, quality review process, and call documentation standard are shared across all seven programs. The script and the qualification criteria change for each campaign type.

Quality review runs across every program. Calls are audited and scored on script adherence, consent handling, and intake accuracy. Agents who miss the standard come off the line until they complete retraining.

The delivery format is standardized. Your team receives a recording, a verbatim transcript, and a structured intake form from every call. You connect your intake system once and receive consistent data packages regardless of program line.

Common questions

How long does onboarding take for a new program?
Onboarding time depends on the complexity of your intake criteria and script requirements. Scripts undergo review before live calls begin. CrashPath provides a timeline estimate during the initial onboarding discussion.
Do agents work all seven programs or specialize?
Agents are assigned to specific program tracks based on training completion and performance scores. A new campaign track requires dedicated instruction before an agent handles live calls.
Who owns the call recordings and transcripts?
Your firm owns the call recordings and transcripts for your campaigns. CrashPath transfers them to your intake workflow as part of every call handoff. Data retention follows the terms agreed upon during setup.
