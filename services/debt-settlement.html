Ethical screening for debt relief campaign lines.

CrashPath qualifies inbound consumer debt calls for relief attorneys and settlement practices. Operators verify unsecured debt amounts, record hardship reasons, and check for active bankruptcy without making financial settlement promises.

Clear hardship screening and balance verification

Debt relief campaigns frequently receive calls from individuals seeking assistance who may not meet minimum program thresholds. Operators work through a structured intake script that verifies total unsecured debt (credit cards, personal loans, medical bills), excludes secured collateral debts, and checks employment or hardship status.

Qualified consumer records transfer directly into your debt resolution software with verbatim audio and full disclosure records attached.

    Disclosure and consent

  3. 3

    Hardship and bankruptcy check

What our operators do not do

Ethical guardrails protecting consumer interests.

  • No settlement guarantees. Operators never promise specific reduction percentages or payoff timelines.
  • No legal advice. Operators do not advise callers to stop paying creditors or provide legal counsel.
  • No pressure tactics. Callers are given clear factual intake with full respect for their situation.
