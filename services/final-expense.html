Structured qualification for final expense campaigns.

CrashPath screens inbound final expense calls against carrier underwriting guidelines. Patient, respectful operators collect age, residency, banking status, and core health criteria before connecting callers to licensed producers.

Patient, methodical screening for senior callers

Senior marketing campaigns require clear pacing, respectful language, and strict qualification checks. Operators follow approved scripts that verify the caller is within eligible age brackets (typically 50 to 85) and has an active bank account for premium debiting.

When pre-qualification criteria match, the caller transfers warm to your licensed agent, or a structured callback is booked with complete caller notes and audio attached.

  1. 1

    Warm greeting and consent

    Operators answer with clear, measured speech, verify identity, and record state-mandated consent.

  2. 2

    Age and residency check

    Caller confirms date of birth and state of residence against carrier program availability.

  3. 3

    Basic health and payment status

    Operators review basic health knockout conditions and confirm active checking or savings account ownership.

What our operators do not do

Protection of consumer choices and regulatory boundaries.

  • No policy sales. Operators do not sell insurance or discuss specific policy premiums.
  • No high-pressure closes. If a caller is unsure or requests time to think, details are saved without pressure.
  • No financial guarantees. Operators collect reported details and pass files to licensed producers.
