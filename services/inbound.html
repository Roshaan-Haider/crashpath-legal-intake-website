CrashPath provides dedicated inbound call center capacity for firms that require live answers at volume. Trained agents follow your screening criteria, document caller information, and route qualified files to your team.

How inbound call campaigns operate

High-volume paid marketing campaigns cannot afford overflow to voicemail. When prospective clients call your campaign lines, our intake operators answer with a 2 minute average answer time, deliver required disclosures, and capture structured responses.

All screening questions follow your firm specifications. If a caller meets your criteria, the file routes immediately to your intake desk. If they do not qualify, a clear disposition note is logged without pressure or debate.

What our operators do not do

We maintain strict boundaries to protect caller trust and legal separation.

  • No legal advice. Operators do not assess legal fault or discuss claim values.
  • No cold outbound calls. Every intake starts strictly from an inbound dial to your firm campaign line.
  • No pressure tactics. If a caller asks to end the call, operators close respectfully and log details.
  • No outcome promises. Scripts strictly prohibit financial projections or settlement promises.
