Structured intake under strict CMS marketing guidelines.

CrashPath screens inbound Medicare inquiries for healthcare agencies. Operators follow Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services rules, deliver mandatory disclosures, verify Part A and B enrollment status, and route callers to licensed advisors.

Clear disclosure and enrollment window screening

Medicare intake campaigns demand strict adherence to federal disclosure guidelines. Every call begins with the required Third-Party Marketing Organization (TPMO) disclaimer before any health plan inquiries are addressed.

Operators record the caller Medicare Parts A and B effective dates, note Special Enrollment Period (SEP) or Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) eligibility, and verify county of residence against plan service areas.

  1. 1

    Mandatory TPMO disclosure

    Operators read the standardized disclaimer within the first minute of the call and log verbal consent.

  2. 2

    Parts A & B enrollment check

    Caller confirms Medicare enrollment status and zip code for county-level plan availability.

  3. 3

    Warm handoff to licensed broker

    Pre-qualified callers transfer directly to licensed insurance agents for personalized plan comparisons.

Regulatory boundaries

Standards required for Medicare marketing qualification.

  • No plan recommendations. Operators do not recommend, enroll, or sell specific Medicare plans.
  • No cold outreach. Dials originate solely from inbound campaign lines.
  • No misleading claims. Operators never claim government affiliation or endorsement.
