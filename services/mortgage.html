Pre-qualification intake for lending campaigns.

CrashPath pre-screens inbound mortgage dials for lending institutions and brokerage firms. Operators capture loan purpose, estimated property value, existing balance, and credit profile before handing off files to loan officers.

Screening for purchase and refinance leads

Mortgage campaigns generate inquiries across a wide spectrum of borrower readiness. Our intake operators filter raw campaign calls against your underwriting parameters: property type, owner-occupancy status, current loan balance, and estimated credit tier.

Borrowers meeting your minimum thresholds are connected live to your licensed mortgage loan originators or booked into calendar consultations with full file notes attached.

  1. 1

    Loan purpose identification

    Operators confirm whether the borrower seeks a home purchase, rate-and-term refinance, or cash-out equity loan.

  2. 2

    Property and equity profile

    Caller reports estimated home value, current mortgage balance, and property jurisdiction.

  3. 3

    Direct loan officer handoff

    Pre-screened leads transfer immediately to your origination team with recording and transcript delivered.

Lending boundaries

Strict separation between intake screening and loan origination.

  • No rate quotes. Operators do not quote interest rates, APR, or specific monthly payments.
  • No credit checks. Operators collect self-reported estimates only; no credit pulls are performed.
  • No loan commitments. Operators pre-screen caller facts and transfer leads to licensed originators.
