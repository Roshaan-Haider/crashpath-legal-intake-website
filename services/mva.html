CrashPath handles motor vehicle accident intake from start to finish. Trained agents answer, screen, and document every detail so your attorneys review actionable case files rather than incomplete notes.

The intake and qualification flow

When a call connects on an accident campaign line, an agent answers with their name and the program line they represent. Before collecting accident details, the agent reads the required recording disclosure. If the caller consents, intake proceeds. If consent is declined, the agent notes the choice and closes the call respectfully.

Every question is structured to record incident details, injuries reported at intake, insurance coverage, and representation status without leading the claimant. The firm receives a structured lead file with all source audio attached.

Handling injured or distressed callers

Callers reaching an accident campaign line often face pain, disorientation, or shock. Agents are trained to pause the checklist when a caller shows signs of distress. The agent acknowledges the situation, verifies the caller is safe, and asks if they want to continue.

If the caller needs to pause, the agent saves the partial record, logs the reason, and schedules a follow-up time if permitted. Incomplete intakes are preserved with all context attached so the caller does not have to repeat their story later.

Agents maintain a calm, objective demeanor throughout. They never offer medical evaluations or legal advice. If a caller asks legal questions, the agent explains that reviewing attorneys make those determinations and redirects the call to the factual script.

What we don't do

  • We do not give legal advice. Agents do not assess liability, offer legal opinions, or comment on claim strength. Any legal question redirects to the reviewing attorney.
  • We do not promise outcomes or settlement amounts. Agents do not quote monetary figures, promise representation, or guarantee compensation.
  • We do not pressure callers. If a caller chooses to decline questions or stop the conversation, the call concludes immediately. We preserve partial details without pushing.
  • We do not contact individuals who have not requested contact. All intake begins from inbound campaign dials or explicit callback inquiries.

Notice: call recording and attorney advertising rules

Call recording consent rules vary by jurisdiction. Certain states enforce two-party consent requirements, while others follow one-party consent standards. Additionally, state bar associations maintain specific guidelines regarding legal marketing and intake disclosures. All scripts and consent statements on this demonstration site are sample templates and must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel in each target jurisdiction before campaign launch. Follow-up through retainer signing

Once a lead file reaches your team, follow-up timing determines claimant conversion. When authorized by your firm protocol, CrashPath agents can perform structured follow-up calls to connect claimants with your legal team or check whether sent retainer agreements arrived.

Follow-up calls continue according to agreed frequency limits until the retainer is signed or the claimant declines representation. If a claimant chooses not to move forward, the file updates immediately with their reason, preventing redundant contacts.
