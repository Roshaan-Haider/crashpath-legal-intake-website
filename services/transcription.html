Transcription standards

Operating rules governing audio capture and transcript handling.

  • No recording without affirmative consent. If consent is declined, recording stops immediately.
  • No audio alteration. Recordings are delivered in raw, unedited form alongside transcripts.
  • No outcome assessments. Transcripts reflect facts reported at intake without legal conclusions.
